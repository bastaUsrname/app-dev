# Breaking Bad

Breaking Bad is my favorite series.  
It is about a teacher who makes drugs to help his family and get rich.

## Main Characters
1. Walter White  
2. Jesse Pinkman  
3. Skyler White  

## Why I Like It
- Good story, good drama and good action from being a teacher to a drug lord type-stuff gives me goosebumps every time the mc stands up.
- Great series production material it feel so real and serious.
- Very exciting, i cant stop watching every time i watch its episodes
